# Safari Smoke, official site — brief (6 Oct 2026)

Fresh start. Nothing from the drive site is carried over: no code, no art, no copy, no mascot renders.

## What it is
One scroll-driven page that headlines the Safari Snaxx gummies and the live-rosin vapes, meets the mascots in the wild on the way down, teaches the products in passing, and lands on the online store.

## Concept: The Watering Hole, with The Drop inside it
- One tall illustrated dry riverbed, top to bottom. Scrolling DOWN moves you DOWN the river. Thumb and world agree.
- Each bend hides a mascot with a product. Tap-to-open ranger notes carry one fact each.
- The Drop: a single gummy tumbles through the whole page, past the mascots, into the jar on the shop counter. Rendered once, scrubbed by scroll.
- The river widens into the pool at the bottom. The pool is the store.

## Rules learned the hard way
1. Two compositions from day one: one portrait master for phones, one landscape master for desktop. Never crop one into the other.
2. Motion direction follows the scroll. Down is down, or towards you. Never away and up.
3. Approved stills, not generated continuity. Every asset is a locked image or a short loop, approved one at a time, filed in the ledger.
4. Assets come from the Higgsfield API only (Jon's surplus credits). No MCP generation.
5. Every reference image shown to Jon before any generation.
6. Every character wears trousers. No bare legs on the cast.
7. The current gummies packaging is a trial run and stays off the site. Show the gummies themselves, the mascots and the flavours, not the bags.
8. The gummy shape is locked to Jon's photos in `refs/gummies/`: a truncated square pyramid (flat square top, wider square base, rounded edges) crusted in coarse sugar crystals. Never a cube, never a bear.
